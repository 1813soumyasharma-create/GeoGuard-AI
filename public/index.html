📚 Third-Year Capstone Project Defense & Viva Voce Guide

Comprehensive geotechnical theory, mathematical formulation, and oral defense answers for examiners.

The standard geotechnical Factor of Safety (FoS) of an infinite slope is:

FoS = [ c' + (σ_n - u_w) · tan(φ') ] / [ γ · z · sin(β) · cos(β) ]

Mechanism of Rainfall Failure: Prolonged rainfall infiltration raises the transient groundwater table, spiking pore water pressure u_w. In Terzaghi's effective stress principle: σ' = σ_n - u_w. As u_w climbs, effective stress drops, destroying frictional shear resistance and causing slope failure.

Deterministic Limit Equilibrium Methods (e.g., Bishop's or Janbu's slices) require continuous geotechnical boreholes, triaxial shear testing, and continuous slip circle geometry for each individual hill profile. While accurate for a single cut slope, it is financially and physically impossible across hundreds of square kilometers.

AI/GIS Advantage: By training on remote sensing factors (DEM slope, curvature, TWI, satellite NDVI, geology), machine learning maps regional watersheds (100s km²) in seconds, pinpointing exactly where civil engineers should prioritize structural mitigation.

Q: Why did you benchmark Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Network, and Logistic Regression?

A: Different models possess different inductive biases. Tree ensembles (XGBoost & Random Forest) handle non-linear feature interactions, mixed categorical/continuous data, and spatial correlations without strict Gaussian assumptions. Logistic regression serves as a linear baseline, while MLP assesses deep feature representation.

Q: Why is drainage considered 80% of landslide stabilization?

A: Slope angle & gravity are fixed, but shear strength is dynamically determined by effective stress. Installing horizontal perforated drains relieves trapped pore water pressure u_w, immediately restoring effective normal stress without requiring expensive retaining walls.
